# Brand specification — Dra. Rainara Campos

## Design direction

The site uses a calm, health-oriented visual system that translates the profile's real work into an approachable digital experience. The tone is attentive, clear, and human rather than clinical or promotional.

## Palette

- **Deep eucalyptus:** `#315c5b` — primary actions, headings, and dark sections.
- **Soft sage:** `#e4ece9` — supportive backgrounds and information strips.
- **Mist:** `#f6f7f5` — page background and negative space.
- **Pale mint:** `#dfece7` — accents, chips, and decorative details.
- **Slate text:** `#1e2b2a` — body and interface text.

## Typography

- **Display:** DM Serif Display, including the italic face for emphasis.
- **Body:** DM Sans, used for navigation, explanatory copy, labels, and CTAs.
